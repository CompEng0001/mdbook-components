Asides

Asides communicate context, guidance, outcomes and problems without interrupting the main narrative. Each type has its own accent and icon. All colours remain readable against mdBook’s light and dark themes.

These colours were selected via: https://chromonyms.com

Types and purposes

DirectiveAccentWhen to use
checklistFlameChecklist
loCaramelLearning outcomes
goalNougatFor what you want to achieve
note, infoBlueAdditional factual context
tipPurpleUseful practice or suggestion
explanationTealWhy something happens or how it works
successGreenA completed action or expected outcome
caution, warningOrangeA potential issue requiring care
bugRedA defect, unexpected behaviour or debugging task
danger,error importantRedA serious risk or destructive action

Collapsible and icon-free asides

Aliases

info maps to note, warning to caution, and error or important to danger. success, explanation and bug are distinct styles, not aliases.

:::

To diagnose a mismatched closing directive, an optional explicit closer may be used (::: /important, ::: /note). Plain ::: remains supported. The parser reports opening lines and nesting depth for unclosed components. Because plain ::: carries no component name, it cannot always identify which intended opening it should match if an intermediate closer is missing.
